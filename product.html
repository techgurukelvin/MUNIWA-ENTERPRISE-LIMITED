Buying guide

Why Foam Density Matters

Density is the weight of foam in each cubic meter (kg/m³). Higher density foam generally has more material in it, which usually means it lasts longer and keeps its shape better under daily use.

Tell us who will sleep on the mattress and how often it will be used, and we will recommend the right density for your budget.

bedHome use: choose by comfort and body weight.
schoolSchools and hostels: choose a firmer, harder-wearing foam.
local_hospitalHospitals and clinics: ask us about washable, wipe-clean covers.
