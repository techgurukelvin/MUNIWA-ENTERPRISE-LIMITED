Who We Are

Built on Quality, Driven by Comfort

Founded in 2021 and proudly based in Ruai, Nairobi, Muniwa Enterprises Limited specializes in manufacturing and supplying high-quality mattresses designed to elevate your sleep experience.

In addition to mattresses, we offer a wide range of products including chemicals, plastic buckets, mugs and plates, chairs, pillows and covers, cushions and covers, bedsheets, blankets, and non-woven bags.

As innovators, partners, and leaders, we are dedicated to delivering products that blend comfort, durability, and superior craftsmanship with a strong focus on quality and customer satisfaction.
